About

Rediscover Sanctuary Cap Cana, the Dominican Republic's most exclusive adults-only all-inclusive resort.

You’ll be delighted by the resort’s $45 million modernization as you explore spectacular suites, artfully crafted lobbies, updated restaurants and bars, enhanced world-class spa and state-of-the-art fitness center.Sanctuary Cap Cana, A Luxury Collection Adult All-Inclusive Resort is located in Cap Cana, a private gated community retreat inside Punta Cana. Cap Cana is made up of 30,000 acres of flawless beaches

Guests can spend slow mornings beside the Caribbean Sea, move between quiet pools and breezy terraces throughout the day, and enjoy evenings shaped by refined dining, premium drinks, live entertainment, and attentive service. The resort is designed for a relaxed adults-only escape where every part of the stay feels polished, private, and easy to enjoy.

The setting also gives travelers convenient access to Cap Cana's marina, golf, beaches, and nature experiences, making it simple to balance restful resort time with memorable local activities.
